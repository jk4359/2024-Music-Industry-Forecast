**Executive summary**
Studio 1FO (1FO) is aiming to expand their operations to the US music market in 2024. Forecast trends in streaming services, music genre popularity, and music marketing strategy.

**Background**
Studio 1FO (1FO for short) is a small music production team based in Seoul, Korea. Since 2020, they have been operating genre-agnostically, sending demos and striking deals with anyone they can come in contact with. Their operations have primarily relied on word-of-mouth marketing and local connections within Korea.
Entering 2024, 1FO plans to take their presence online and globally, with the key objective being to build a consistent brand and establish a stable venue for music sales.
1FO is seeking data insights about the music industry to target prominent streaming platforms and music genres for its brand. Their primary target is the US music market, but they would like to make their content globally accessible to countries outside of Korea and the US.

**Project Breakdown**

Project 1: Streaming Services & Platform Trends
What music streaming services are most used?

Project 2: Music Genre Trends
What genres are popular?

What individual artists or groups (e.g., K-pop idols) are showing high growth rates? 
- How are they marketing themselves? (SNS, local events and performances, etc.)
